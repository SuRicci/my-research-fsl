\lhead{Technical note: early draft}
\begin{abstract}
Unlabeled galleries may contain many classes outside a few-shot episode. We examine a gallery-only transfer of established open-set prototype fitting with frozen visual features and queries excluded from adaptation. In two image-disjoint Oxford-IIIT Pets pools, source-default matched one-shot accuracy is 94.92\% and 94.87\%, below retrieval-weighted controls at 96.24\% and 96.02\%. Mean inlier ranking AUROC is approximately 0.93, yet about 80\% of retained gallery weight is task-external. Retrospective final-step mass balancing removes most mismatched-gallery harm but leaves a matched one-shot deficit. Oracle composition filtering produces a separate diagnostic gain using unavailable inference labels. The results distinguish ranking quality, retained mass and composition within a fixed transfer protocol. They establish neither a new algorithm nor a failure of original transductive OSLO or independent-domain generalization.
\end{abstract}
